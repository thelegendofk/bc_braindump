# Proposal Quality Checklist

A pre-submission gate. Run it before handing the draft to the review skills. Each
item is a yes-or-no. A no is either fixed or consciously accepted with a reason.

## Narrative

- [ ] The executive summary follows Situation-Complication-Resolution.
- [ ] The Complication makes clear why acting now matters.
- [ ] The Resolution answers the Complication directly.
- [ ] Every section leads with its conclusion, then gives support (pyramid).

## Titles

- [ ] Every section and slide title is a full sentence with a verb.
- [ ] Reading only the titles gives a coherent argument.
- [ ] Numbers in titles are all supported in the body.

## Structure

- [ ] The client's situation comes before the firm introduction.
- [ ] The firm introduction sits with or after Why us, as proof.
- [ ] If an RFP mandates a structure, it is followed exactly.
- [ ] There is a clear next step at the end of the commercial section.

## Evidence

- [ ] The proposal quantifies business impact where a real number exists.
- [ ] Reference clients and case studies are real and cleared for use.
- [ ] No invented number, saving, certification, or reference appears anywhere.
- [ ] The Why us section has three or four concrete differentiators with evidence.

## Commercial

- [ ] Pricing is presented, with the required currency, tax note, and validity.
- [ ] Where useful, two or three tiers are offered with a recommended middle.
- [ ] A short risks and mitigation table is present.

## Form

- [ ] Main body is within the target length, detail is in appendices.
- [ ] One consistent visual system across the document (and across Word and deck).
- [ ] Every figure, table, and diagram has an action caption.
- [ ] House conventions hold: no em-dashes, Swiss spelling (ss not ß), real
      umlauts, English written for non-native readers.

## Separation of concerns

- [ ] No win-strategy or compliance-matrix content was rebuilt here (that is
      `shipley-proposal`).
- [ ] Internal working notes and the internal business case do not leak into the
      client draft.

## Handoff

- [ ] Ready for `tech-executive-review` (buyer read), then `red-team-review`.
